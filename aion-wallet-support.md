> **Only download from this GitHub release page.**

Legitimate help for Aion Wallet comes from documentation and public issue trackers, never from a person who contacted you first, and no support path ever needs your seed phrase, keystore file or password.

That rule is worth more than any single answer below, because the most expensive losses in self-custody do not come from software faults. They come from a holder handing credentials to someone who sounded helpful.

## You'll see this if

- You have a problem the guides do not cover.
- Someone has messaged you offering to fix a wallet.
- You are looking for an official channel and finding several.
- You need to report a bug and are not sure what to include.

## Start with the symptom

Most questions have a written answer. Access problems are covered by the [Aion wallet login options](https://github.com/aion-wallet/aion-wallet-help/blob/main/aion-wallet-login-options.md) guide, empty balances by the zero-balance article, and connection stalls by the sync guide. Reading the matching page first is faster than waiting for a reply.

The full set is in the [index of every guide](https://github.com/aion-wallet/aion-wallet-help), grouped by what the reader is trying to do.

## Public issues, not private messages

The place to report a defect is the issue tracker of the repository that owns the code. For the wallet that is the desktop repository; for staking behaviour it may be a network question rather than an application one.

A good report contains the version, the platform, the exact wording of any error, and the steps that reproduce it. It never contains a phrase, a private key, a keystore file or a password, and it never contains a screenshot with any of those visible.

Private messages that arrive first are the pattern to distrust. A real maintainer answers in public, where the answer can be read by the next person with the same problem.

## What support can actually do

A maintainer can confirm whether a behaviour is a bug, explain how a feature is meant to work, and fix the application.

A maintainer cannot reset a password, restore a phrase, reverse a transaction, recover funds sent to a wrong address, or read your balance without your address. Those limits are properties of a non-custodial wallet, not choices.

If someone claims otherwise, the claim itself is the warning. The [Aion wallet forgot password](https://github.com/aion-wallet/aion-wallet-help/blob/main/aion-wallet-forgot-password.md) article explains why no reset exists.

## Reporting a security problem

If you believe you have found a vulnerability, describe the impact and the affected version without publishing a working exploit, and avoid testing it against wallets you do not own.

Do not send funds to demonstrate a bug, and do not paste a phrase into a proof of concept. A report that requires either is not a report worth making.

## If you are locked out

Locked out is not a support case in the usual sense. It is one of three situations: a forgotten wallet password, a lost keystore password, or a missing backup. Each has a fixed set of options, and none includes a recovery service.

Anyone who offers to recover a wallet for a fee is either guessing passwords against a file you send them or taking the fee and disappearing. There is no third possibility.

## What a good question looks like

A useful question states the version, the platform, what you expected and what happened. That is enough for a maintainer to reproduce the behaviour, and it is far more likely to receive an answer than a message that begins with a demand.

Include the exact wording of an error rather than a paraphrase. The precise text is often the fastest route to the cause, which the [Aion wallet not syncing](https://github.com/aion-wallet/aion-wallet-help/blob/main/aion-wallet-not-syncing.md) article demonstrates with connection errors.

Do not include screenshots with an address, a balance or a keystore filename visible. A public issue is public forever.

## Prevention

Keep the installer you used, and note its version. A support question is much easier to answer when the version is known.

Write down what you did before the problem appeared: an update, a restore, a new account. The sequence usually identifies the cause.

Never let a support conversation move to a channel you did not choose. The move is the technique, not the message.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there an official support email?

There is no channel that can act on your account, and any address claiming to be support should be verified through the project's own pages before you use it. The safest route is the public issue tracker of the relevant repository.

### Will support ask for my seed phrase?

Never. A request for a phrase, a keystore file or a password is a theft attempt regardless of who appears to send it.

### Can support reverse a transaction?

No. A confirmed transaction is part of the chain and cannot be undone by any application or team.

### What should I include in a bug report?

Version, platform, exact error text, and reproducible steps, with no credentials and no identifying screenshots.
